### Added

- chat: **the assistant can now read its own trading desk** — five new tools give chat full read visibility over the autopilot, which previously it had none of: `trader_config` (active book, whether autonomous execution and real-money orders are armed, kill-switch/pause files, sizing, caps, stop basis, brakes and cooldowns), `trader_journal` (realized P&L, max drawdown with its dates, headline *and* risk-exit-only win rates, expectancy, profit factor, MFE/MAE, per-exit-path breakdown, disclosures), `trader_skips` (every declined opportunity by reason — counts only, never an implied P&L), and `trader_alerts` (rules + fired feed). Ask "what is the trader set to", "how did my week go", or "why isn't it trading" and it answers from the machine instead of guessing.
- chat: **three non-financial capabilities** — `trader_alert_create` and `trader_alert_delete` (manage watchlist alert rules in conversation; validated by the same allowlist as the UI), and `trader_pause`, which is deliberately **one-way**: chat can stop the autopilot but can never start or arm it, because stopping is risk-reducing and wants to be sayable fast while arming stays a gated action with its own UI. Pausing tells the user their open positions and broker-side protective stops remain in place.
- The money boundary is unchanged and now regression-tested: no chat tool places, sizes, or arms an order — Act stays behind `lib/trading-guard.js` and the ADR-0020 gates.
