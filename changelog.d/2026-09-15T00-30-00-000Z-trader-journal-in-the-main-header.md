### Changed

- **Trader and Journal are both in the site header.** The switch between them used to be a pair of tabs inside the trader's own toolbar, so the journal had no way back to the charts, and from anywhere else on the site it was two clicks deep and unnamed. They sit side by side in the header now, one click from every page, with the header lighting whichever one you are on. The trader toolbar keeps only what is about the chart in front of you.
