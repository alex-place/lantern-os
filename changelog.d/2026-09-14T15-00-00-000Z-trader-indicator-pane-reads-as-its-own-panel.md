### Changed

- trader: **an indicator pane reads as its own panel.** The line between the price chart and an indicator strip was a faint solid rule in the grid colour; it is now dotted, in its own tone, and runs the full width, price scale included. The strip's values sat a column to the left of the price scale; they now line up with it, and the strip has a real scale: nice ticks on the same 1/2/5 ladder as the price axis (as many as its height allows), with its own faint gridlines under the lines. The separator colour is still yours to change in chart settings.
