### Added

- trader-ui: drawings are editable the way TradingView's are. Selecting one raises a floating toolbar (settings, colour, width, delete), and its gear opens a dialog with Inputs / Style / Visibility tabs whose fields differ per tool — a Long position asks for account size, risk and lot size; a fib retracement asks whether to extend and what to label. Fib levels are multicoloured by default instead of one flat purple, and positions honour their own target and stop colours. Every value saves on the drawing (#3354)
