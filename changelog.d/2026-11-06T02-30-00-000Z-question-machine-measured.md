### Added

- question machine: an information-gain selector, and the machine's first two measurements.
  - `QuestionMachine.ask_by_information` chooses the experiment with the most exact expected information gain per unit cost, among those CAP and NAP admit. It is G2's selector, ported from lantern-os-research (research PR #17). A refused experiment is never chosen. Its worth is still reported, and when only refused experiments would inform, the stop reason is `denied`.
  - The wrong-prior benchmark (`experiments/question_machine_wrong_prior.py`) starts the machine from a deliberately wrong model. The shipped `reconcile` notices the contradiction, but the seam-ranked question never picks the experiment that decides (0 of 200 worlds). The benchmark's own information-gain policy, which estimates the gain by Monte Carlo rather than through `ask_by_information`, does (99–100%), except when the truth is none of its hypotheses and its effect is small. It then claims the nearest hypothesis in 78–96% of those worlds.
  - 20 Questions on BED-LLM's Animals targets (`experiments/question_machine_20q.py`), with gpt-4o-mini, on 30 of the 100: the machine solved 20, the paper's Prompt-Only baseline 18. That is no significant difference (sign test p = 0.79), at 22× the cost per game. See §8 and §9 of `docs/research/question-machine.md`, and the new row in `docs/BENCHMARKS.md`.
- ci: the Sigma0 runtime job now also runs these three suites.
