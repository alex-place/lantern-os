### Fixed

- Trading clocks follow the NYSE calendar (lib/market-calendar.js). Weekday holidays are closed all day, and a 13:00 early close (the Friday after Thanksgiving; July 3 and December 24 on Monday-Thursday) ends the session at 13:00. Before this, the two-sleeve runner and the server's autoscan treated every weekday 09:30-16:00 as open. The scans read the Yahoo feed's after-hours prints (13:00-15:55) as session bars. The 15:45-15:55 rules fired after the close, where a market DAY order cannot fill: the leg's de-carry, Friday's eod_flat, C's max_hold and C's and O's closing decisions. A 3x position open at 13:00 on 2026-12-24 would have been carried Thursday to Monday. On an early close those rules now move with the close (de-carry 12:50-13:00, C's 12:45/12:50 bars, O's 12:50 bar, the leg's last decision before 12:50), session bars stop at 13:00, and max_hold counts real sessions. Full days are unchanged. The rules reproduce the replay caches exactly: all 102 weekday closures and all 21 early closes from 2016 to 2026. Unscheduled closures go in MARKET_CLOSED_DAYS / MARKET_EARLY_CLOSE_DAYS.
