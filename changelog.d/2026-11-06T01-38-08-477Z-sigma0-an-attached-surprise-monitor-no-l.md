### Fixed

- sigma0: an attached surprise monitor no longer fails the batch on one broken trajectory. SurpriseMonitor factored every row's S = CΣCᵀ + R in one batched Cholesky, so one row whose covariance held a NaN made forward_step raise for every row. A row whose prediction, covariance, observation, C or R holds a NaN or inf is now not scored: it never reaches the factorization, its NIS reads NaN, so it never spooks (a row at +inf could read NIS = inf and spook), and its posterior is its prior. A NaN NIS is no evidence anywhere it is read. It stays out of sigma0_proximity's rolling mean, where it read as proximity 1.0 (an alert), and out of info["surprise_nis"], and verify_with_surprise no longer counts it as a consistent reading that raises confidence. Batches whose rows are all finite are computed exactly as before. A finite row whose S is not positive definite still fails the batch. See §3 of docs/SIGMA0-COLLAPSE-CERTIFICATE.md.
