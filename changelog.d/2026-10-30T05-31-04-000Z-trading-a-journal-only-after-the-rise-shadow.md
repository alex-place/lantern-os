### Added

- trading: a journal-only after-the-rise shadow (TRADER_RISE_SHADOW=1, off by default). Every entry the day-trader places gets a rise_shadow_entry row with its name's prior-session rise in trailing standard deviations (the prior close-to-close return over the sd of the 20 daily returns before it) and whether a skip-after-a-big-rise rule at 1.5 sds would have refused it; one rise_shadow_check row a session (from 09:00 ET) lists every scanned tradelist name's reading. In the replay such entries lost money on the recent windows and not on the older quarters, so it is not a rule; the shadow lets the live record answer it. No order, sizing or gate changes; the rows carry the user rise-shadow and stay out of every account view. The example engine configs pin it blank.
