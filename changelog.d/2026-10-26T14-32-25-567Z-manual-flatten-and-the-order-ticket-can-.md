### Fixed

- Manual Flatten and the order ticket can cover a short. A buy that the book shows only brings a short back toward flat (qty no more than the short, less resting buys) now clears IBKR's order warnings automatically, as a risk-reducing sell already did. It also tells the broker guard it reduces risk, so the position-size cap cannot block it outside regular hours. Before, covering stable's accidental XLE short (-2,628) dead-ended at 'IBKR returned order warnings' from both the Flatten button and the ticket. The same check now reads the position's sign: it used |qty|, so a sell against a short counted as risk-reducing and would have auto-accepted doubling the short. A warned buy that cannot be verified now says so in IBKR's own words instead of 're-submit with acceptWarnings:true'.
