### Added

- trading: the stable server's day-trader can park the cash its gross cap keeps in one T-bill fund (lib/reserve-fund.js), the engine's cash reserve (#3788) driven by env knobs: TRADER_RESERVE_FUND_PCT (absent or blank = off), _SYMBOL (BOXX), _BAND_PCT (10), _MIN_CASH_PCT (5), _WINDOW (12:00-12:30 ET). One decision per session, the operator's armed book only. It reads cash two ways, IBKR's cash field (which reads low after the day's buys) and equity minus the marked positions (which reads high on a feed dropout): buys are sized from the lower, and the fund is sold to restore the cash floor only when both are under it. The day-trader never touches the fund (excluded on the scan and fast-exit passes alike) and never counts it as open risk: the concurrency cap, the correlated-risk buckets, the gross cap, slot_util and the re-protect guard all leave it out. The IBKR order guard caps a reserve order (reserve: true, a T-bill fund only: BOXX SGOV BIL SHV USFR TFLO TBIL) at 90 % of equity instead of the trader's per-position cap; the flag on any other symbol is refused. The shared reserve now sends the price and equity it sized from with each order, reads an IBKR order warning (needs_confirmation) as not placed, and takes its ownership book as optional. With stable's gross cap at 60, a fund at 35 % leaves at least 5 % in cash.
