### Added

- trading: a written plan for every entry the brain places, journal only. Off until a sleeve's env sets `TRADER_TRADE_PLAN=shadow` (no other value turns it on; nothing acts on a plan in this build). One `trade_plan` row lands after each placed entry, under the user `trade-plan` so no P&L reader counts it: the thesis (the sleeve's signal: the noise leg's band break, the close-IBS washout, the overnight book above its trend, the month-end bond book, or the brain's own entry; the signal's reading; and the gamma and news context read from the entry tags' shared files, with `tags_ref` = the order id), the expected path (the planned exit time from the sleeve's own rules and the expected move by then: the median |return| of the traded fund over the same clock window in the 14 prior sessions, at least 10), `if_expected` and `if_wrong` (the sleeve's exits and stop as configured), and `if_neither`, the in-between branch: checkpoints (the leg at +30 / +60 / +120 minutes before 15:50, close-IBS at the next session's 10:00 / 12:00 / 14:00, the month-end book at +1 / +2 sessions) with the expected move by each and the levels a time/progress rule would read. Its action is HOLD: no such rule passed a static test on the armed design's replays, so the branch is recorded to be scored forward. The overnight book has no in-between (the market is closed between its 15:55 buy and its 09:40 sale). Never awaited; a failed bars read still writes the row with its reason; a pass more than 10 minutes off the real clock (a replay) writes nothing unless `TWO_SLEEVE_FAKE_NOW` is set (the runner's dry rehearsal).
