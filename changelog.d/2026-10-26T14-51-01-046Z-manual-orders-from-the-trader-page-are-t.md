### Changed

- Manual orders from the trader page are the user's decision. The ticket, Flatten, Close-all and Rapid-mode orders accept IBKR's precautionary warnings on the user's confirmation, buys and sells alike, and the toast shows what IBKR said. They are not judged by the engine's position-size cap. The arming switch, the real-money opt-in, the halt file and the 100,000-share sanity ceiling still apply. Flatten now means flat: the server re-reads the position fresh at the broker (IBKR's portfolio cache invalidated) and closes exactly what is held, less any closing order already working. If the position is already flat, on the other side of what the page showed, or unreadable, it sends nothing and says why. Live 2026-10-26: after the XLE short had been covered, a Flatten sized from a stale -2,628 bought another 2,628 (long about $181k), and the next Flatten was refused. The engine's own orders are unchanged (P0-8).
