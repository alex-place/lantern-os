### Fixed

- Trader: a position the trader has already sold can no longer be sold a second time while the broker's position read still shows it. On 2026-11-17 and 11-18 the stable box (IBKR paper) sold XOP and JNUG twice each and went short, because the positions read lagged the sale by one to three minutes: a fresh stop (or the re-protect step) was placed on shares that were already gone. A sale of ours is now remembered per symbol, and every sell-side step skips that symbol until the read reflects the sale, the exit order shows dead, we buy the symbol again, or the hold has passed (TRADER_SOLD_HOLD_SEC, default 600 s for an accepted order and three times that for a booked fill; 0 = off). Journal rows sold_guard / sold_guard_expired. Test: stale-position-double-sale.test.js.
