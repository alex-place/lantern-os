### Fixed

- Day P&L is the account's equity change since the previous close. The trade log only knows the longs the trader entered and exited, so the rebuilt figure drifted from the account's real day on most sessions: median $607 over 51 stable sessions, worst $10,491. The causes are holdings the log never recorded, short positions it read as long exits, and commissions. On 2026-10-26 the page said -$899 on a +$1,627 day. The tile now shows equity minus the previous close (Alpaca last_equity, or the trader's own session row for IBKR, matched to the previous trading day by the calendar). Realized and the open-position move keep their trade-log meaning, and a new Other figure names the rest, so the three still add up; the Day P&L tooltip shows all three. The IBKR tile also gets a day figure when no positions are open, instead of IBKR's dpl, which resets around 17:15 ET. The daily session record adds account_day_pnl and prev_equity beside the book's own day_pnl.
