### Added

- trading: the stable server gets the engine's overnight book (sleeve O, #3741) as lib/overnight-book.js: SPY, QQQ and SMH are bought at the 15:55 print when the name's prior close is above its trailing 200-session mean, and sold at the 09:40 print of the next session; weekends are held. It runs createOvernightScan's own windows and gate, places its orders through the account's facade (market, day, accepting IBKR order warnings), and reads no market data outside 15:55-16:00 ET unless it holds something to sell. Env knobs: TRADER_OVERNIGHT_BOOK_PCT (per name, % of equity; absent or blank = off, 0 = sell what is held and buy nothing), TRADER_OVERNIGHT_BOOK_SYMBOLS (SPY,QQQ,SMH). The operator's armed book only, regular-session passes only, one buy attempt per name per session, a sale resent only when the broker refused it. One owner per name: the book never buys a name the account holds at 15:55, and the day-trader leaves the book's names alone from the buy until two positions reads show the sale gone. The book buys only into the room the day-trader's gross cap leaves, and the day-trader counts the book's notional toward that cap, so the two together keep the cash floor.
- trading: the day-trader's partition now also keeps partition rows (the reserve fund and the overnight book's names) out of its book tracking and its confirmed holds, so a sale of three of them at once no longer reads as a feed dropout that holds entries, and a partition sale in the orders feed is never booked as the trader's own exit (its order id is remembered).
