### Added

- trader: **engine alerts** (#3248, #3250) — per-user alert rules evaluated inside the existing ~1/min autoscan (zero extra market-data calls): *signal fired* (bullish/bearish/any), *price at zone* (within a chosen % of the computed support/resistance), and *washout confirmed* (the engine's Σ₀ ENTER verdict — raw IBS isn't carried on scan rows, and the verdict is the engine's own stricter form of the same question). Fired alerts land in a per-user in-app feed with a per-rule quiet window (cooldown is server-stamped — a client edit can't reset it), rules are capped at 20 per user with strict allowlist validation, and evaluation is fail-soft so a bad rule can never break a scan. New **Alerts tab** on the terminal (deep link `/stock-trader.html#alerts`): rule builder, rules list with enable/disable and delete, and the fired feed; guests see the Pro teaser. Email + web-push delivery is the follow-up (#3249) — in-app feed ships first.
