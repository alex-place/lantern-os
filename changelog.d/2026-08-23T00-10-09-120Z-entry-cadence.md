### Added

- TRADER_ENTRY_CADENCE_MIN (default off): new entries are decided only in the first minutes after a bar boundary (phase/window knobs), the way every validating lab read the signal, instead of at the first 60s scan where the session IBS pokes through 0.30. Cadence lab: on 20 sessions of 1-minute data with identical rules the engine analog is payoff 0.57 / −0.2%; entries confirmed on an hourly close (exit unchanged) are payoff 0.68–1.64, 8 of 9 boundary phases beat the engine (mean ≈ +3.7%) with a lower drawdown in every case; the 2y hourly labs (which sample :30 closes) make +23%/+25% per half. Payoff lab: the loss side is 53–76% stops; hold-underwater, scale-out, close-based stops and loser time-stops are rejected (they raise payoff by trading worse or fail holdout); the ratchet trail's hourly/daily gains collapse on 5-minute bars (bar artifact).
