### Fixed

- trader: **the journal books recovered fills on the broker's clock, not the server's.** A protective stop that filled while the server was down was journaled at the moment the next boot *discovered* it — an IWM stop that filled Wednesday 14:56 ET rendered as "Closed Fri, 11:27 AM", and every date-derived stat (streak, expectancy, worst-day attribution, weekday×hour) inherited the two-day lie, while the page footer swore "broker-accepted fills only" (#3660, finding C). Both booking paths now stamp the row with the broker's own execution time — the orders-feed reconciler passes through the `filled_at` it already parsed for its age guard, and the per-order stop-status sweep now receives `filled_at` from the Alpaca lookup that previously discarded it. A `discovered_at` field keeps the booking moment observable, and a fill with no broker time books at now() exactly as before. Findings A/B from #3660 (the exit-sequencing race and its silent error) are deliberately not in this change.
