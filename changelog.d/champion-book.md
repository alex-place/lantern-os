### Added
- **Champion allocation book — live on the paper Alpaca account (ADR-0027/0028).** `lib/champion-book.js` deploys the backtested long-horizon Champion: a momentum-tilted, shrunk-tangency allocation across a fixed 8-ETF universe (SPY/QQQ/IWM/EFA/TLT/GLD/XMMO/SPMO), scaled by the streaming vol-target brake's live gross (`brake-monitor` grossTarget, 0–2×, → cash in storms), rebalanced with a no-churn drift band. `GET /api/trading/champion` returns the plan (target weights × gross, current-vs-target, drift orders); `POST /api/trading/champion/rebalance` rebalances the PAPER book. **Governance:** PAPER ONLY (refuses a live account), **DRY BY DEFAULT** — places orders only when BOTH `?arm=1` AND `CHAMPION_ARM=1` ("a person decides"); real capital stays gated on the ADR-0028 Sharpe-CI mandate (unmet). Every rebalance appends to `champion-book.jsonl` so the gate can score the paper track record. Pure math (`tangencyDir`/`targetWeights`/`computeRebalance`, ported from `tangency_dir`) unit-tested (7). This is Act, but slow-and-diversified — NOT the day-trader; do not arm it on an account the day-trader also trades (it targets its own 8-asset mix and would sell everything else).
