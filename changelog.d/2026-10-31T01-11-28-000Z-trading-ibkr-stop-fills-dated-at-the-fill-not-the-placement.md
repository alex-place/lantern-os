### Fixed

- trading: an IBKR stop that fills days after it was placed is no longer booked to the day it was placed. IBKR's order list reports `lastExecutionTime_r` on a GTC stop as its submission (or last modify) time, so GDX (stop placed 10-22, filled 10-23) and GLD (stop placed with its 10-29 entry, filled 10-30) were stamped the day before, dropped out of their own day's session record (10-30 showed stops_fired 0 with the GLD stop) and sent per-trade P&L matched by timestamp to the wrong trades. The brain now remembers when each broker order was last seen working (kept across restarts); a fill whose reported execution time is no later than that is stamped at its discovery, a scan or so after the fill, and keeps the broker's value as `broker_time` with `ts_from: "discovered"`. Fill times that come after the last sighting (Alpaca's `filledAt`) still win, and an order never seen working is dated as before.
