### Added

- markets: **a page for every market, the way TradingView has them.** `/markets.html` is now the hub — a market summary (the S&P 500 drawn large beside the major indices), then US stocks, crypto, futures and forex — and every heading and "See all" leads to that market's own page at `/market.html`: indices (US and world), US stocks (sector performance across the eleven SPDR sector funds, breadth, gainers, losers and most active, with pre-market and after-hours rankings), crypto (sixteen coins by market cap), futures (energy, metals, agricultural, index and Treasury) and forex (major, cross and emerging-market pairs, the dollar index, and a cross-rate grid). Each board has an Overview and a Performance view (1W–1Y), and every instrument opens its own symbol page with a chart, its statistics and returns. Every board is ONE batch request to Yahoo, in a module that shares nothing with the armed trader's quote path — the movers board this replaces priced its 119 names one chart call at a time. What can be misread is labelled: grains in US cents, a derived cross rate, sixteen coins that are not "the crypto market", a curated list that is not the exchange. Also: Block trades as XYZ (SQ had stopped pricing), GDX is no longer counted as a leveraged fund, and leveraged funds are labelled "leveraged" rather than "3x" (UCO, NUGT and JNUG are 2x).
