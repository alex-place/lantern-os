### Changed

- trading: the journal-only entry tags now carry the TYPE of the news (lib/entry-tags.js). Each Benzinga headline goes through the research's keyword categories (DATA, FED, TRADE, GEO, POLICY, HEALTH, CORP, and WRAP for market-reaction content). Each window of an entry_tags row gains `types` (NEWS = not WRAP, SHOCK = TRADE|GEO|POLICY, RATES = DATA|FED: count, rate, rank against the 120 sessions before, tercile) and `counts` for the rest, and the row gains `mix` (which of SHOCK and RATES had more session headlines). The research found the close-IBS sleeve's buys pay on shock-news sessions and not on rates-news ones; that was found in-sample, so this builds the forward record. No order, sizing or gate changes, and no extra API call: the same refresh keeps the headline text. The history is re-seeded by scripts/entry-tags-news-seed.js; a recent row without type counts is counted again by the next refresh.
