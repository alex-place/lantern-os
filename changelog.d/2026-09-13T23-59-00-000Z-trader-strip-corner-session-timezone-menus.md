### Changed

- trader: **the strip's corner, the way TradingView has it.** The RTH/ETH button opens a menu — **Regular** (9:30 AM to 4:00 PM ET) or **Extended** (4:00 AM to 8:00 PM ET, pre-market and after-hours) — two rows because that is what the feed serves (Yahoo's chart data stops at the extended session; a 24-hour row is one line the day a feed carries the overnight session). The clock is now a button that opens a **timezone** menu: the exchange, your device, UTC, the trading cities with their offsets, and a search box over every zone the browser knows; the clock and the chart axis read in the chosen zone (the same setting as Settings › Symbol › Timezone, which now accepts any real zone). The **market state** (open / pre-market / after-hours / closed, with the countdown) stands beside the clock as its own readout, and the **VIX** moved down from the header to sit with it. Every button says on hover what it is on — "Regular — 9:30 AM to 4:00 PM ET", "Timezone — Exchange (New York)" — and the range buttons read in words: "1 day in 1 minute intervals".
