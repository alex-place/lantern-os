### Security

- trading: a signed-out visitor can no longer change the watchlist every signed-out visitor shares. Watchlist add/remove is exempt from the trade gate so free accounts can edit their own list, but a request with no session has no user id, and the store filed its write under `default` — the one list every signed-out visitor is shown on the public trader page. Anyone without an account could add symbols to it or empty it for everyone, and each add put one more symbol on the server's collector rounds. Signed-out adds and removes now get a 401 ("sign in to keep your own watchlist"); reading the starter list stays open, and signed-in accounts, free ones included, edit their own list as before. The trader and explore pages say so instead of failing quietly, and a chart slot changes only once its symbol is on the list.
