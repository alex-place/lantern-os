### Changed

- The hero is now the introduction alone, full width, and the engine's verdicts moved below it as a grid of six compact panels — each with the symbol's live price track, its stop and targets drawn on it, win probability, expected value and either the targets or the plain-English reason it was refused — followed by a View all tickers button into the terminal. The panels deliberately mix four accepted with two turned down rather than ranking purely by expected value, which would have filled every slot with accepted setups and buried the refusals.
