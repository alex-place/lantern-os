### Fixed

- profile/nav UX (2026-07-16 audit, findings 52/53). **(#2653)** Locked feature cards on `profile.html` were inert `<div>`s (`pointer-events:none`, opacity `.45`) — a user at peak upgrade intent ("Trader · Requires Pro") had the click swallowed and the requirement text sat below the contrast floor. They are now `<a href="/pricing.html">` links (click no longer swallowed), dimmed at opacity `.6`, with a visible **"Upgrade →"** affix on the requirement line — a direct path to pricing from the moment of intent. **(#2654)** On phones ≤420px the injected plan badge (`.nav-tier`, variable-width text) plus the four fixed-width nav action buttons overflowed the actions row and clipped the wordmark — the same crowding `site.css` already fixed for app navs, reintroduced by the badge. The badge now collapses to a 14px tier-colored dot (label hidden via `font-size:0`; the color cue, the click → `/pricing.html`, and the title tooltip are all kept). Verified in the dev preview against the live stylesheets: the `.nav-tier` dot measures 14×14 / border-radius 50% at 375px, and a rendered locked card resolves to an `<a>` whose click targets `/pricing.html` at opacity `.6`.
