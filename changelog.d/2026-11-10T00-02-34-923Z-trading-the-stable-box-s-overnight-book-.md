### Added

- trading: the IBKR bar fallback for the stable box's overnight book (`TRADER_BARS_IBKR_FALLBACK=shadow|1`, off unless set, IBKR accounts only). When Yahoo's newest 5m bar ended more than two minutes ago inside a regular session (the 10-30 miss), the book's bars get IBKR's newer ones from `/iserver/marketdata/history`, through the same fallback the engine uses for Alpaca's (`lib/two-sleeve/bars-fallback.js`; the engine's behaviour is unchanged). Every IBKR request goes through one pacer: one at a time, 1.1 s apart, at most 12 a minute, none for 15 minutes after a 429, refusing rather than queueing, because IBKR's rate limit can hold the session the orders use. The shadow never holds a pass (its read runs detached) and journals what it would append; armed, it refuses a batch whose first new close is more than 20 % from Yahoo's newest (a wrong price scale). Three times a session (10:05, 12:05 and 15:52 ET) a `bars_ibkr_probe` row records, for SPY, QQQ and SMH, what IBKR serves: its `mdAvailability` and `mktDataDelay`, its newest bar and that bar's lag, and the close/high/low gaps against Yahoo's same bars (and against Yahoo's bar one step earlier, which shows how IBKR stamps a bar). The paper account had no market-data subscription when this was built, so the probe answers whether IBKR's bars are usable there at all. Rows go to the stable journal under the user `bars-ibkr`. New read-only `IbkrCpapi.getHistory(conid)`. Nothing here places, cancels or changes an order.
