### Added

- trading: **the two-sleeve engine core (#3656).** One account, two brains, symbol ownership, per-sleeve environments, one tick order — as a driver-agnostic module (`lib/two-sleeve/engine.js`, `lib/two-sleeve/ownership.js`) that the faithful replay harness and a headless live runner both drive. The validation gate is literal and it passed: driven by the replay bars the core reproduces this morning's teamwork sweep trade for trade — the S-first blend 9.91% / drawdown 1.72% (169 of 169 trades identical), the race-first blend 10.14% (173 of 173), the decarry blend 3.27% (213 of 213), each sleeve alone likewise. Getting there found one real bug: releasing a symbol dropped its order tags, so a sleeve's filled protective stop fell to the default sleeve and the owner read its own stop-out as an external close (one SOXL trade then hit a stale max-hold clock and sold ten minutes in, 1.2 points of return). Tags now live until the broker stops listing the order, and a test pins it. The runner (`scripts/two-sleeve-runner.js`) boots both brains with their own journals and state, resolves the broker facade, takes the account lock as the armed holder, and in `--dry` mode journals every order and places none; booted dry against the live box it resolved the account and stood down on the lock held by the live process, as designed. Nine contract tests (`test/two-sleeve-engine.test.js`), a config carrying the measured design (`scripts/two-sleeve.config.example.json`), and `docs/two-sleeve-engine.md`. Nothing on either box changes; the next gate is a dry session on a real account, then paper.
