### Changed

- trader: **the PAPER badge moved from the header to the strip's left corner**, on the range buttons' line, the way TradingView's "Paper Trading" sits on its trading panel. Clicking it hides or shows the account panel below (positions, orders, order history) and the choice is remembered; the chevron beside it opens the account menu — **Trading settings…** (the settings dialog's Trading tab) and **Broker settings…** (Settings › Connections) — and turns up while the menu is open. The demo badge keeps its wording; guests without the demo see no corner.
