### Changed

- trader: **the ☰ menu is gone, and so is everything that was hiding in it.** Pre-market/after-hours trading moved to **Settings → Connections**, beside the two switches it belongs with (which broker executes, and whether the autopilot runs at all) — it is account-wide server state, not a chart control, and it is now an explicit Regular hours / ⏰ Extended choice rather than a button you had to click to discover. The Kalshi link, the broker pill and the Chat button were all second routes to somewhere you could already get: Kalshi from Explore, the broker page from the account corner menu's "Broker settings…", and the chat rail from its own dock tab. The keyboard shortcuts that lived in the menu are now written down in the Trader Guide, which also documents Shift+B / Shift+S rapid orders for the first time.
