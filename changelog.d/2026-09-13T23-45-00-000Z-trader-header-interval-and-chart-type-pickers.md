### Changed

- trader: **the interval and chart-type pickers stay in the header, the way TradingView's toolbar has them.** The favourite intervals sit inline with the current one lit; the ▾ opens the whole list grouped Minutes / Hours / Days with a star on every row to promote or demote it, so the toolbar is the reader's own set. The same for the chart type, which grows from line/candles to **Bars, Candles, Hollow candles, Line, Area and Heikin Ashi** — each a few lines in the one renderer, nothing the chart cannot honestly draw. Favourites are kept on the device and on the account (`preferences.headerFavs`, carried in the session like the indicators). The Draw button and its header flyout are gone: the drawing tools live on the rail to the left. Line is now unfilled by default since Area is the filled one (Settings › Symbol › Line still has the fill switch).
