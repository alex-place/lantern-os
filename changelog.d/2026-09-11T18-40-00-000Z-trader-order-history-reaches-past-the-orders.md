### Fixed

- trader: **Order history no longer sits empty on an account whose orders predate what `/v2/orders` returns.** A connected Alpaca paper account showed 8 long-held ETF positions while both the Orders and Order-history tabs said "None" — `/v2/orders?status=all` answered with nothing, and the route silently fell through to the (wiped) local ledger. Order history now falls back to Alpaca's canonical execution record, `/v2/account/activities?activity_types=FILL`, whenever the orders list comes back empty — so the history tab shows the fills that actually built the book. The fallthrough also stopped being silent: the route logs whether `/v2/orders`, FILL activities, or nothing answered, so an empty pane is diagnosable from the server log instead of indistinguishable from a broken adapter.
