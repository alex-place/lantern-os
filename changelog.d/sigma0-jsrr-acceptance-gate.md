### Changed
- Σ₀ serve loop: the generation acceptance gate now consumes the **JSRR discrete spectral-radius criterion** ρ(A)<1 — the stability object externally validated on real looped LLMs (STARS, "Stabilizing Recurrent Dynamics for Test-Time Scalable Latent Reasoning in Looped Language Models", arXiv:2605.26733), and the criterion appropriate to a *discrete* iterated loop. New `cio_sde.collapse.jsrr_certificate` computes the exact discrete spectral radius of the empirical exit-depth Jacobian plus STARS' single-step power-iteration surrogate `‖Av‖₂²` (their Eq. 3, reusable as a training regulariser); `Sigma0LoopLM.generate()` accepts a latent trajectory iff ρ<1 (band tunable via `SIGMA0_JSRR_MARGIN`, default 0.0 = STARS' literal ρ<1). The prior #768 *continuous* region-wideners (`proven_contracting`) — a stricter, over-rejecting sufficient condition — are retained as diagnostic telemetry and as the acceptance fallback when JSRR is unavailable. JSRR is numpy-only, so it gates acceptance even in the minimal inference venv where scipy (the continuous Lyapunov/Kreiss legs) is absent. Verified by `tests/test_sigma0_jsrr_gate.py` (12 cases: exact-ρ verdicts on synthetic contraction/rotation/divergent operators, margin band, nan-safety, agreement with the `experiments/sigma0_jacobian_stability` matvec twin, and the `_stability_gates`/`_accept_stability`/`assemble_reason_verdict` wiring); `test_cio_sde`, `test_stability_gates`, `test_reason_verdict`, `test_collapse_canary_e2e` unchanged and green. The `SIGMA0-COLLAPSE-CERTIFICATE.md` write-up of this gate is deliberately deferred until the grounding-honesty study that pairs with it is researched and tested.
