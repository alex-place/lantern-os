### Changed

- trader: **an indicator pane is its own chart.** MACD, RSI and the other strip indicators now have a scale of their own that you can zoom — drag or scroll the gutter beside the strip — and pan, with time always following the price pane; double-click the gutter to go back to auto. **Drawings work inside a pane** (a trend line on the MACD is anchored in MACD units, hit-tested and dragged there, and never leaks into the price pane). **Drag the line above a pane** to give it more or less of the chart; the height is remembered with the indicator. The crosshair reads the pane's value below the price pane. And **every colour of an indicator is editable**: each plot has its own swatch (MACD and Signal, %K and %D, ADX and both DIs, a band's upper/middle/lower), the MACD histogram's up and down are their own two, and "Reset colours" brings the indicator's own back.
