### Added

- markets: **the US economy gets its page** (`/market.html?c=economy`), the last of TradingView's market sections: inflation (CPI, core CPI, core PCE), jobs (unemployment, payrolls, jobless claims), growth (GDP, industrial production, retail sales, housing starts, consumer sentiment, the trade balance) and rates (the fed funds target range, the effective rate, the 2- and 10-year Treasury yields and their spread, mortgages), plus the Treasury yield curve now, a month earlier and a year earlier. Every figure is FRED's republication of the agency's release, printed with the month, quarter or day it is AS OF and a link to its series; a change carries a neutral arrow, never green or red, because whether a rise is good depends on the figure. The hub gains an Economy section. Six small requests per refresh, one per native frequency (FRED answers a mixed-frequency request with a ZIP, not a CSV). United States only, and no release calendar: there is no free source for one.
