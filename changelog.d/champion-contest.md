### Added
- **Paper-trading contest — one shared pool across Free / Pro / Pilot (ADR-0028).** `lib/champion-contest.js` + endpoints under `/api/trading/champion/contest/*` let any signed-in user start/stop a virtual Champion book with a goal and compete on one leaderboard. Each book is a VIRTUAL paper portfolio — at start it "buys" the Champion target mix (`champion-book.targetWeights` × the streaming brake's gross) with a fixed $100k, then is marked to market against REAL prices on every read; no per-user Alpaca, no real orders, no real money, so the pool is inclusive of every tier. `POST …/contest/start` (body `{goal}`) / `POST …/contest/stop` (auth required); `GET …/contest` (my book, live-marked); `GET …/contest/leaderboard` (public, ranked best-return-first with Free/Pro/Pilot badges + per-plan counts). Pure math (`buildHoldings`/`markBook`/`rankBooks`/`planBadge`) unit-tested (5). `planBadge` also works around a plan-matrix gap where the `pilot` role has no `ROLE_TO_PLAN` entry (flagged separately). Stacked on the champion-book engine (#2707).
