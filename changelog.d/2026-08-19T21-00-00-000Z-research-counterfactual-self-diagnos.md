### Research — counterfactual self-diagnosis, and the oracle that makes world S readable

The mistake ledger is killed, so the self-model gained a second mechanism: replay every DESIGN
round already paid for under an alternative cost exponent and ask whether it would have picked
the observable that survived. Paired, discordant rounds only — a world with no cost trap produces
no disagreement, so there is nothing to fire on, which is where the ledger version failed.

New: `run_cost_exponent_sweep.py` measures what the repair is worth, so a world-S null can be read
correctly. On 200 seeds of world H the ladder runs 20% → 11% → 5% of seeds buying the cheap proxy,
truth committed 89% → 92%, with slightly fewer experiments. The repair is worth making, so a
failure in world S is a failure of diagnosis, not of the repair.
