### Fixed

- sigma0: an independent review broke the first version of the certificate fixes, and these are its fixes. The acceptance verdict no longer accepts a loop whose hidden states went non-finite, and reads the whole loop rather than its last step, so a period-2 map with ρ>1 no longer passes. Rotations and Jordan blocks no longer read as collapse in any basis or next to a large mode, the discrete dichotomy's Schur split now reads complex eigenvalues by modulus, and a batch's bounds cover every member. The Question Machine's plans respect `u_max`, and `reconcile` no longer says "act" on a denied plan, on a mirror's answers, or because `dt` is small. `QuestionDrivenLoop.run` now takes `observed=(states, controls)`, the controls actually applied. `LinearDynamics` rejects negative noise, and an armed step refuses a negative diffusion gain, which breaks the state kick's escape guarantee. See Appendix C of `docs/SIGMA0-COLLAPSE-CERTIFICATE.md`.
