### Fixed

- ci: **the hourly deployment check no longer cries wolf while production is intentionally offline.** `deployment-verify` was created on 2026-08-14 to turn a silent outage into a notification within the hour. It worked — and then failed every hour for four days without a single green run, because the GCP project had been suspended for billing and production was down on purpose. An alarm that is permanently red is not an alarm; it is training to ignore the alarm. `ops/production-status.json` now carries a `paused` flag with a reason and a date, and the **schedule** honours it. Explicit intent is never paused: a manual `workflow_dispatch` or a post-release run still verifies, because there someone is asserting that a host should be serving right now. Flipping `paused` back to `false` re-arms the hourly tick.
