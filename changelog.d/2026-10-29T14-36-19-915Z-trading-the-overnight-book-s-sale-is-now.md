### Added

- trading: the overnight book's sale is now priced by the broker. On its first sale (stable, 2026-10-29) the journal held only the quotes the orders went in at, so the book's P&L could only be estimated. The overnight_book_gone row of a sold name now carries fill_px and filled_qty from the sale's own order status (the per-order read, which answers for any id the book names), cost_px (the position's average price at the sale, which is the book's own buy fills; the buy quote when the broker reports none, named in cost_from), and ret_pct and pnl against it; the sell row carries cost_px too. A sold name waits up to three more reads for the broker to price its sale before it is released, then goes with fill_px null and fill_why; a broker without a per-order status releases at the second read, as before.
