### Changed

- trading: **the entry-time separator study, and what it found.** Fifteen features captured at entry on every replayed trade of both traders' armed configurations; the search reports only buckets that lose in *both* halves of the window, because the 15:00 block taught that a bucket over-represented among losers can still be net positive. Result: no filter worth arming on either trader — and a sharp picture of where the income lives. Race's entries within five minutes of the session low are a third of its trades carrying 57% of its return at a 78% win rate; stable's entries after a five-day drop of 2.23% or more are a quarter of its trades carrying 78% of its return at 84%. Mean reversion pays on the multi-day stretched washout, and the actionable form is sizing, not selection. Two default-off grafts on race's brain follow from it, measured *through the brain* on the armed 13-name universe: fresh-low sizing ×1.5 (12.27% → 15.33% at identical drawdown; the first version failed silently because `sizePosition` takes the minimum of three legs and only two were scaled) and a Friday entry skip (win rate 71% → 76%, positive weeks 79% → 86%, worst week −9.0 → −5.2, since race carries over weekends). Together: 15.83% at 2.34% drawdown with 86% positive weeks — the best race configuration measured. Stable's equivalent is parked: its hard per-position cap forbids the size, by the operator's own 08-25 decision. A daily 5-minute bar collector now runs at 16:35 so candidates can be judged on an out-of-sample window in about six weeks.
