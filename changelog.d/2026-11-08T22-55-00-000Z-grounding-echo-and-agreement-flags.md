### Added

- memory: every CSF memory write now says who produced it, in fields the schema already has: `actor_type` (`user` / `agent` / `system`), `actor_id`, and `metadata.provenance` (`user` / `self` / `system`). A council answer and an agent signal are unisona's own output (`self`), a life fact is the user's, an order is a system event. Until now every record was stamped `actor_type: "system"`, so nothing could tell the system's own claims from outside evidence. `isSelfAuthoredRecord()` reads this, and falls back to `content.role` for older records.
- grounding: `GROUNDING_EXCLUDE_SELF=1` keeps unisona's own earlier output out of the local-memory grounding ring: its recalled chat turns, the council's stored answers and its agents' signals. Counted as corroboration, the system's own past answer plus one other hit read as two independent witnesses under the noisy-OR. On the local ledger (555 past questions, each replayed with only the turns before it), 117 drew on the assistant's own turns and 53 "grounded" answers rested on nothing else. Off by default; the user's own turns and outside records still count.
- council: `COUNCIL_STRICT_GROUNDING=1` stops the answerability gate treating agreement as grounding. An uncontested reply with no execution check and no anchor that makes claims (groundedness-canary assertiveness at or above `COUNCIL_STRICT_ASSERT_MIN`, default 0.2) gets `seam_open` (or `pin` when nothing external is reachable) instead of `grounded` by `low_delta`. Replies that assert nothing, anchored replies and execution-checked ones are unchanged. Every review now records `agreementOnly`, `strictGrounding` and the reply's assertiveness, so the effect can be measured before the flag is turned on; on the local ledger it would change 4 of 415 verdicts. Off by default.
