### Added

- memory: every CSF memory write now says who produced it, in fields the schema already has: `actor_type` (`user` / `agent` / `system`), `actor_id`, and `metadata.provenance` (`user` / `self` / `system`). Trading signals (from `lib/trading-memory.js`, the live writer) and council answers are unisona's own output (`self`); orders are system events; life facts are the user's (`actor_id` stays empty until a caller passes a server-resolved id). Until now every record was stamped `actor_type: "system"`, so nothing could tell the system's own claims from outside evidence. `isSelfAuthoredRecord()` reads the stamp and recognises older records by their shape (a self `content.role`, or a trading signal): on the local registry that is 376 of 910 records (371 signals, 5 council answers).
- grounding: `GROUNDING_EXCLUDE_SELF=1` keeps unisona's own earlier output out of the local-memory grounding ring: its recalled chat turns, stored council answers and agents' signals. Today an earlier reply recalled by keyword overlap becomes the evidence itself, with its overlap score as its confidence, so on its own it can clear the resolver's answer threshold and be cited as grounded evidence. In a replay of the local conversation log (each question seeing only the turns before it), 18 of the 25 ordinary chat questions the ring grounded rested only on the assistant's earlier replies. The filter runs before the top-k cut, so a relevant user turn ranked just below the assistant's own still counts. It also changes what the node serves to mesh peers (`/api/mesh/ground` uses the same ring). Off by default.
- council: `COUNCIL_STRICT_GROUNDING=1` stops the answerability gate treating agreement as grounding. An uncontested reply with no execution check and no anchor that makes claims (groundedness-canary assertiveness at or above `COUNCIL_STRICT_ASSERT_MIN`, a value in (0, 1], default 0.2) gets `seam_open` (or `pin` when nothing external is reachable) instead of `grounded` by `low_delta`. Replies that assert nothing, anchored replies and execution-checked ones are unchanged; autowork's PR body and Approve warning say "unverified: claims backed only by agreement" for these instead of "contested". Every review now records `agreementOnly`, `strictGrounding` and the reply's assertiveness, so the effect can be measured on real traffic before the flag is turned on (the local dev ledger can't: its agreement-only verdicts are almost all a health probe's replies, too short to score). Off by default.
