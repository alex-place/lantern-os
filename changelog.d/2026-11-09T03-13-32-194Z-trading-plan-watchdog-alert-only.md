### Added

- trading: the plan watchdog (`TRADER_PLAN_WATCH=alert`, off unless set; alert only). On every brain pass, from the pass's own broker reads (no extra call), each open trade plan of the sleeve is checked against its position: still held 3 minutes after its planned exit (a down-day carry the brain recorded moves the time to the next session's de-carry), no working sell order covering the shares when the plan listed a stop (3 passes running), a quantity off the plan (2 passes) or a short, a position gone with no exit row (3 passes), an exit by a rule its plan did not list, and a held position with no plan. Each condition is journaled once when it starts (`plan_alert`, user `trade-plan`, level fault or info) and once when it clears (`plan_alert_cleared`, with the minutes it lasted); a fault also appends a line to `plan-watch-ALERTS.log` beside the sleeve's journal (`TRADER_PLAN_WATCH_ALERTS` moves it). A relaunch rebuilds what it watches from the journal, so nothing is said twice. With the watchdog on, the brain also journals a `plan_carry` row the first time a day the down-day carry holds a leg trade overnight. Nothing here places, cancels or changes an order.
