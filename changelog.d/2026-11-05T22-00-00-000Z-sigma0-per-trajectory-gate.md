### Changed

- sigma0: the anti-collapse gate now decides per trajectory, and the kick lies in `null(A)`. The four signals are each sample's own (its gradient, the rank of its own Jacobian, its covariance's anisotropy, its control sensitivity), and only the samples whose own four conditions hold are kicked, bumped or overwritten, each with its own receipt (`InterventionReceipt.sample`) and its own intervention budget. As far as the operators go, a batch is now B independent trajectories: before, a collapsed member could hide among healthy ones, and `e₁e₁ᵀ` and `e₂e₂ᵀ` each triggered alone but not together. In numbers a batched run agrees with its rows run alone to rounding, bit for bit only where nothing rounds differently: `Tensor.std` rounds one row differently from the same row in a batch, and the neural `Dynamics`' matrix products do too (about 1e-15 in float64), so a row within rounding of a threshold can decide differently. Each sample's kick and covariance bump use the right singular vectors of its own `A` for its small singular values, where the old subspace came from the symmetric part and, for non-normal `A`, pointed partly along directions the drift moves. Theorem C3 and Lemma S1 are unchanged and now hold per trajectory. `AntiCollapseOperator.proximity` returns one value per sample, and a surprise spook raises only its own trajectory. The state kick's gain guard (`g·dilation` finite and non-negative, from #3811) now checks every kicked row, including when one gain is shared by all rows. See §3 and Appendix C of `docs/SIGMA0-COLLAPSE-CERTIFICATE.md`.
