### Fixed

- trader: **drawings stay on their bars when the chart moves, and a click on any part of a drawing selects it.** Anchors used to map to pixels by elapsed clock time across the loaded window, so a weekend or an overnight inside the window pushed every drawing sideways by dozens of bars, by a different amount each time you panned. Anchors now map through the bar index, in one place shared by the painter, the click, the handles and the drag (a body drag slides along the bars, so ten bars is ten bars across a weekend). The hit test used to measure strokes and single anchor points; it now measures what each tool draws: a rectangle's, ellipse's, triangle's or channel's fill, a fib's intermediate levels, a regression band, a position's boxes, a label's letters. A line crossing a box still wins when you click on the line.
