### Added

- trading: the two-sleeve engine can park a share of equity in one cash-like fund (a top-level "reserve" block, off unless configured; lib/two-sleeve/reserve.js). The paper account pays no interest and the engine's book is mostly cash: at live size the replay's overnight book held a median 17 % and never more than 58 % of equity. The fund (intended: BOXX, whose T-bill yield accrues in its price, so a paper account captures it) is reserved to a pseudo-owner RESERVE in the ownership registry, so no sleeve sees, adopts or sells it (an unclaimed position was adopted by the default sleeve S, whose exits would sell it), and it is added to every brain's excludeSymbols. It decides once a session in a quiet window (default 12:00-12:30 ET), trades only outside a band around pct of equity, never buys below a cash floor, and sells to restore the floor when the sleeves hold more than usual. The engine journal gets reserve_ready / reserve_plan / reserve_order rows; ownership_adopted rows now also name each symbol's owner.
