### Added
- **Sigma Trader runs itself: monthly-rebalance + brake schedule (ADR-0028).** `lib/sigma-scheduler.js`, started from `server.js` alongside the streaming brake, drives the Sigma Trader on its own cadence — one market-hours check (`SIGMA_SCHEDULE_MS`, default 20 min) whose no-churn band self-regulates BOTH rhythms: slow momentum drift (≈ monthly in calm markets) and a fast brake-driven de-lever when `brake-monitor`'s gross steps toward cash in a storm. It ONLY places orders when the market is open (so they fill, not queue), the engine is armed (`SIGMA_ARM=1`), and Sigma has its own dedicated account — otherwise it idles and reports why. Fully independent of the day-trader's autoscan (separate engine, separate account — they never contend). Off by default (`SIGMA_SCHEDULE=1` to enable); a `min-gap` floor (`SIGMA_MIN_REBALANCE_MS`, ~55 min) stops a jittery brake over-trading. Status is surfaced on `GET /api/trading/sigma-trader` (`schedule` field). Tests: `test/sigma-scheduler.test.js` (7 — off/armed/plan-only/market-closed/no-account/on-target/weekend).
