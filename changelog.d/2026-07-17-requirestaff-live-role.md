### Fixed

- auth: `requireStaff` now gates on the **live persisted profile role** (`effectiveRole`), not the role snapshotted into the cookie at login (#2627). The #2627/#2605 fix — re-derive role per request so a demoted admin/tech_support or a downgraded/charged-back tier loses access on the *next* request — was applied to `requireRole`, `isStaff`, and `isAdmin`, but `requireStaff` was missed and still trusted `session.role`. It has **no live call sites** today (the `/api/accounts/*` staff surface gates via `isStaff`, which was already correct), so this is latent, not an active hole — but it's the lone gate still carrying the stale-role pattern, and a future route adopting `requireStaff` would silently reintroduce it. Added `apps/lantern-garage/test/live-session-role.test.js` pinning the whole gate family (`requireRole`/`isStaff`/`isAdmin`/`requireStaff`): a stale-admin cookie over a demoted profile is denied on the next call. Verified the guard has teeth — reverting the `requireStaff` line makes exactly that assertion fail.
