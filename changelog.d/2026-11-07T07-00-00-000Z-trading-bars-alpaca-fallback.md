### Added

- trading: a fallback for the two-sleeve engine's custom scans (overnight, close-IBS, month-end, noise leg) when Yahoo's 5m bars run late inside the session, as on 2026-10-30 when the 15:50 bar never settled before 16:00 and both the overnight book and the close-IBS decision missed. Off until a sleeve's env sets `TRADER_BARS_ALPACA_FALLBACK`: `1` appends Alpaca's real-time 5m bars (the free plan's IEX feed; `TRADER_BARS_ALPACA_FEED=sip` on a plan with the real-time tape) after Yahoo's newest bar once that bar ended more than two minutes ago; `shadow` reads Alpaca the same way and only journals what it would append (`bars_fallback_shadow`). Yahoo's bars are never changed or dropped, an empty or failed Yahoo read is left as it is, one request serves every symbol of a scan, and a failing read waits a minute (`bars_fallback_failed`, no key in the row). The worker's `scan_worker_ready` row reports the mode and feed as `barsFallback`.
