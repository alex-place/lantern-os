### Added

- trading: journal-only market-context tags on every live entry (TRADER_ENTRY_TAGS=1, off by default; lib/entry-tags.js). After each entry the day-trader places (and each overnight-book buy) one entry_tags row records the prior session's dealer gamma (SqueezeMetrics' free GEX, ranked against its 252 sessions before: LOW / MID / HIGH tercile and sign), the news intensity (Benzinga headlines on SPY/QQQ/SMH/DIA/IWM per hour overnight and so far in the session, each ranked against the 120 sessions before) and the VIX change at the entry (QUIET / MILD / FEAR at fixed cut points, with the quote's own time and its measured lag). They are measured exactly as the 11-01 research measured them, so the forward record can test those findings; none is a rule. The row is written after the order and never delays it; a failed source is null with its reason. Both boxes share one cache directory (TRADER_ENTRY_TAGS_DIR); only a process holding the Alpaca server keys refreshes the news counts (a few calls a session), and the history is seeded from the research archive by scripts/entry-tags-news-seed.js. No order, sizing or gate changes; the rows carry the user entry-tags. The example engine configs pin both knobs blank.
