### Changed

- trading: the trade plan (`TRADER_TRADE_PLAN=shadow`, journal only) is version 2: it states every rule that can close the trade, with its level, and what usually happens. `exits` lists each rule as configured right now: the noise leg's band exit with the proxy's noise band at every half-hour decision clock and the wrapper price that roughly matches, the broker stop, the max-loss cap with its price and whether it also sells in the extended hours (`in_replay: false` when it does, because the replay never sells there), the late-reversal gate at 15:30, the de-carry, the down-day carry (marked not applicable before a weekend), the Friday weekend flat (it becomes the planned exit when it starts first), the close-IBS max hold, the overnight book's 09:40 sale and the month-end calendar. `scenarios` reads the sleeve's own journal: its last closed trades (up to 120) by the branch that closed them, with the share, the mean result, the win rate and the median minutes held. `today` says whether the entry is a re-entry in the same fund that session and what the earlier tries made. The in-between checkpoints name the band line each one faces. Extra reads per entry: the sleeve's own journal (entry and exit rows only) and, for the noise leg, the proxy's live and settled 5m bars.
