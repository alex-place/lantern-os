### Changed

- trader-ui: the trader now has a right-hand dock. The watchlist moved off the left, and the order ticket, chat and a new drawing palette became panels of the same dock, switched by an icon rail on the far right — one panel at a time, TradingView-style. Closing the dock gives its 300px straight back to the chart. Chat is the panel it opens on by default, and every panel closes. On phones the dock is a bottom sheet rather than a squeezed column (#3355, #3356)
