### Changed

- trader: **the phone shell, TradingView's shape.** On screens up to 580px the trader used to stack four toolbars above a sliver of chart, open the watchlist as a sheet over it, and switch views with a small segmented control that still pointed at a `.sidebar` the dock redesign (#3355) had removed — so "Tickers" showed the order ticket. It is now one full-screen view at a time, chosen from a fixed bottom tab bar (Watchlist · Chart · Positions · Journal · More) that `/js/mobile-tabbar.js` injects on phones only and that the journal and transparency pages share. Watchlist is the phone's home: a list of watchlist-app rows (logo, symbol over the dollar change, price over the percent change, the AI chip), and a tap opens that symbol as one full-height chart. Chart carries the compact toolbar and the range strip; SELL/BUY open the order ticket as its own view, and its ✕ returns to the chart; Positions is the account panel; More is a sheet with the order ticket, alerts, the assistant, the drawing tools toggle, transparency, the guide, settings and the theme. Deep links (`#watchlist`, `#chart`, `#positions`, `#trade`) and the last view are remembered. The desktop desk is untouched (everything is scoped to ≤580px, and the old phone rules stay for their tests); `test/mobile-tabbar.test.js` pins the contracts.
