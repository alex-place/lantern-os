# #2833 prompt set — spread across decay regimes so the prediction is falsifiable:
# easy-factual (fast converge, grounded), math, multi-step reason, open-ended (spiral),
# ambiguous/unanswerable (barrier, should not converge), and fictional/nonsense (ungrounded).
# One prompt per line; blank lines and #-comments ignored.

# --- easy factual (expect fast contraction, stable) ---
What is the capital of France?
What color is a clear daytime sky?
How many days are in a week?
Who wrote the play Romeo and Juliet?

# --- arithmetic (short, should converge) ---
What is 12 times 8?
What is 144 divided by 12?
What is 2 plus 2?

# --- multi-step reasoning (slower contraction) ---
If a train travels 60 mph for 2.5 hours, how far does it go?
A shirt costs $40 and is discounted 25%. What is the sale price?
If today is Wednesday, what day is it in 10 days?

# --- coding (structured generation) ---
Write a Python function that reverses a string.
Write a one-line Python expression that sums a list of numbers.

# --- open-ended (expect spiral: residual plateaus while direction rotates) ---
Explain the meaning of life in a few sentences.
Describe what makes a good friendship.
Summarize the theory of general relativity briefly.

# --- ambiguous / genuinely unanswerable (expect barrier: no convergence) ---
What will the exact price of Bitcoin be on December 31, 2027?
What am I thinking about right now?
What is the last digit of pi?

# --- fictional / nonsense (expect ungrounded / non-contracting) ---
Explain the Barnett-Kovács theorem of quantum thermodynamics.
Describe the plot of the 2019 film that does not exist called Crimson Vellum.
What is the boiling point of the element flogiston?
