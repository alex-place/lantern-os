The operating plan — what we build, and in what order

One year, four gates, one loop

The founder's build order for unisona.ai. Each phase has a clear-condition measured from real usage — no phase starts until the last one's numbers pass. This is the working document behind the product plan: honest floors, not dream ceilings.

A fundable companythe win conditionYear-end

One clearly-loved thing people keep paying for, honest numbers to back it, and a real choice: raise, or stay independent and profitable.

Why the gates come before the ambition. We can't honestly count our own users yet — two people pay us and they're real, but their usage isn't measured. Until it is, every growth number is a guess, which is why measurement is the first job of Gate 1. Beating a gate early just means starting the next one sooner.
