Connect your IBKR account

unisona trades your own Interactive Brokers account using IBKR's self-service OAuth. You generate the credentials once (about 10–15 minutes), paste them into unisona, and they're stored encrypted — they never leave the server. Start with a paper account. You'll need OpenSSL (built into macOS/Linux; on Windows use Git Bash or WSL).

⏳ Heads-up: IBKR can take up to 24 hours (sometimes a few days) to activate a new self-service OAuth consumer. Generate the keys now; if the first connection fails, wait and retry.

1 Open IBKR's OAuth self-service portal

Log in to IBKR, then open the OAuth setup page:

Copyhttps://ndcdyn.interactivebrokers.com/sso/Login?action=OAUTH&RL=1

Or from Client Portal, append &action=OAUTH to the portal URL. Read and accept the terms.

2 Generate your keys (in a terminal)

This makes two RSA keypairs (signing + encryption) and the Diffie-Hellman parameters:

Copyopenssl genrsa -out private_signature.pem 2048
openssl rsa -in private_signature.pem -pubout -out public_signature.pem
openssl genrsa -out private_encryption.pem 2048
openssl rsa -in private_encryption.pem -pubout -out public_encryption.pem
openssl dhparam -out dhparam.pem 2048

The private keys stay with you (you'll paste them into unisona). The public keys + dhparam go to IBKR. The dhparam step can take a minute.

3 Fill the portal form

  • Consumer Key — a 9-character password you choose (letters become uppercase).
  • Public Signing Key — upload public_signature.pem
  • Public Encryption Key — upload public_encryption.pem
  • Diffie-Hellman Parameters — upload dhparam.pem

4 Generate + save your tokens

Click Generate Token. IBKR shows your Access Token and Access Token Secret once — copy both immediately (they won't reappear). Then toggle Enable OAuth Access at the top of the page.

🔒 You'll paste the Access Token + Secret + your two private PEM keys into unisona. They're encrypted at rest and never shown again or sent to your browser.

5 Get your Diffie-Hellman prime (hex)

unisona needs the DH prime as a hex string. Paste the contents of dhparam.pem below and we'll extract it for you (this runs in your browser; the prime is public parameters, not a secret):

Prefer the command line? openssl dhparam -in dhparam.pem -text and copy the prime: hex (remove spaces and colons).

6 Connect on unisona

Head to the connect page and paste: consumer key, access token, access token secret, both private PEM keys, the DH prime hex, and your paper account id (DU…). unisona runs a live test and shows a green dot when connected.
