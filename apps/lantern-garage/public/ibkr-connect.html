How to get your credentials (one-time, in IBKR)
  1. Log in to IBKR Client Portal → Settings → API → Third Party / Self-Service OAuth.
  2. Register a consumer — you'll get a consumer key, access token, and access token secret.
  3. Generate two RSA private keys (private_signature.pem, private_encryption.pem) and upload the matching public keys to IBKR.
  4. Note the Diffie-Hellman prime (hex) from your dhparam.pem.
  5. Use your paper account id (DU…) to start.
DU… = paper, U… = live
