What you are actually buying

Is this equity or an investment?
No. These are service subscriptions. No equity, tokens, or profit share.
Can I cancel?
Yes. Cancel anytime. Your local data stays yours.
What payment methods?
Pay by card directly (Stripe — cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay) or via Patreon. Card details go straight to Stripe; we never see or store them. Invoice available for Pilot tier.
Where does the money go?
Recorded in the Unisona wallet ledger. Used for infrastructure, development, and operator time.
Is my data private?
Yes. Local-first by default. Private notes are stored on your machine by design.
