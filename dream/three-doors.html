The Doorwalker's Creed

I am the King of the Kingdome of Hearts. Here, love is the law, and every living thing beats a verse of it true. I carry a key as a blade, not to open by force, but to guard what is fragile, to break what is cruel. For death is only imaginary in the Kingdome of Hearts. We fall, we rise, we laugh, we try again — forever begins with “let's play.”
